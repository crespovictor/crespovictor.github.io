## Introduction

Designed in 1968 by [Lance Wyman](https://lancewyman.com/brand-project/mexico-city-metro-system-graphics/), the Mexico City Metro's visual identity has become inseparable from the city itself. The system was conceived with inclusivity in mind, making navigation intuitive for everyone, including non-Spanish speakers and those with limited literacy.
That same year, Wyman brought a parallel vision to the [1968 Olympic Games](https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/mexico-city-1968/logo-design), developing a cohesive graphic identity that guided visitors through venues using [bold, universal wayfinding](https://www.olympics.com/en/original-series/episode/design-focus-mexico-1968).

Drawing from pre-Hispanic traditions, Wyman and his team wove together contemporary design and ancient symbolism to craft a visual language all its own, pairing each station with a distinctive icon and name that reflects the cultural and historical depth of the city.

## Movilidad Integrada (Integrated Mobility)

![Mosaic of Movilidad Integrada logos]({{ site.baseurl | relative_url }}/assets/img/ptv/Mosaico_MI.svg "Movilidad Integrada Logos")

In 2019, the government of Mexico City created the "[Sistema de Movilidad Integrada de la Ciudad de Mexico (MI)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movilidad_Integrada)" - Mexico City's Integrated Mobility System to integrate (almost) all systems of public transport within the City and Metropolitan Area in a single unified entity. To further improve this unification, a single payment system was introduced at the same time: the [Tarjeta de Movilidad Integrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarjeta_de_Movilidad_Integrada)

This new integrated system was created to merge the operation of the different public transport systems:

- [Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City_Metro)
- [Metrobus (BRT)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City_Metrob%C3%BAs)
- [El Insurgente (Commuter Train)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Insurgente)
- [Cablebus (aerial lift)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cableb%C3%BAs)
- [Light Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xochimilco_Light_Rail)
- [Trolleybus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trolleybuses_in_Mexico_City)
- [Ecobici (Public Bike Sharing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecobici_(Mexico_City))
- [RTP and Concesionados (Public buses)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_de_Transporte_de_Pasajeros)

## Giving Transport Victoria a new visual identity

What if Transport Victoria had the same identity as Mexico City's Movilidad Integrada?

### DISCLAIMER: I am not a graphic designer and I know I've done mistakes. Please be gentle.

![Mosaic of Transport Victoria Integrated Mobility]({{ site.baseurl | relative_url }}/assets/img/ptv/mosaic_TV.png "Transport Victoria Logos")

Th original MI mosaic and logo includes the colours of 8 different transport systems. However, in Melbourne I could only find 8 systems integrated in Transport Victoria, that's why Metro is included twice on the top and bottom of the mosaic.

This design experiment was done following inspiration from [PTV/Transport Victoria brand guidelines](https://www.righttoknow.org.au/request/5149/response/13973/attach/4/PTVH2977%20MSG%202018%202.4%20Colour%20v10%20PA%20v2.pdf) and [Movilidad Integrada brand guidelines](https://www.semovi.cdmx.gob.mx/storage/app/media/Manuales/2024/Manual%20MI%202024.pdf), merging both into one graphical identity.
